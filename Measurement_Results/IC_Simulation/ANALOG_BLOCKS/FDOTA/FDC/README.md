# FDC characterization scope

The differential core provides the FDOTA forward gain. Its common-mode control input is driven independently to separate signal amplification from common-mode regulation.

- Differential excitation measures gain, phase, offset, noise, and transient common-mode coupling.
- A control-voltage sweep checks output common-mode sensitivity and control polarity.
- Common-mode AC excitation characterizes the plant seen by the feedback amplifier.

Differential and control-sweep fixtures use 40 pF per output. The separate common-mode plant fixture uses 10 pF; its frequency response must be interpreted at that load.

These nominal design checks do not replace integrated FDOTA stability, noise, or transient verification.
